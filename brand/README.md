# TRAILsuite brand

The brand concepts for **NODAtrail**, **CULItrail** and **APERtrail**, and the
logo and icon artwork drawn from them.

> **All three ship.** NODAtrail joined CULItrail and APERtrail in `packages/` in
> August 2026. The concepts below are the briefs the three products were built
> from, and the sub-headlines are what each one does.

**These are 1024px reference copies, not the masters.** They were the masters
until September 2026, at 1536 and 2048 pixels and between 6 and 15 MB each: 55
MB of a 24 MB repository, in a tree that is cloned by anybody who wants to read
the code. The masters live with Technosoftware's other artwork now.

Downscaled with Lanczos and re-encoded, so they are still worth drawing a
smaller copy from, and still deliberately not what a README embeds: a package
that needs a logo keeps its own sized copy under its `images/`.

**The old files are still in the git history**, because taking them out of the
tree does not take them out of a clone. That is the price of having committed
them, and it was judged not worth rewriting the history to recover.

## The concepts

### 1. NODAtrail: The Life OS

* **The Mark:** The icon is a glowing, three-dimensional geometric node (resembling a low-poly brain or a floating core). A sleek, luminous "S-curve" trail cuts directly through the center of the node, wrapping around it like a data stream or a planetary ring.
* **The Color Palette:** The core node is deep charcoal, while the winding trail glows in vibrant **Cyan and Electric Purple**.
* **The Typography:** **NODAtrail** set in the exact same clean sans-serif font as APERtrail. "NODA" is crisp white, and "trail" is a muted, icy blue.
* **The Sub-headline:** *NODAtrail: Map Your Mind. The unified Second Brain to connect your thoughts and navigate your life.*

### 2. CULItrail: The Culinary Manager

* **The Mark:** The icon features a modern, minimalist chef's knife pointing right (indicating forward movement). The sleek back edge of the knife blade seamlessly extends and loops into that signature "S-curve" winding trail. Along this trail, three glowing dots (nodes) represent the steps from meal, to order list, to plate.
* **The Color Palette:** We maintain the appetizing colors from your previous choice. The knife and trail are a metallic charcoal, while the three nodes on the trail glow in a warm **Saffron Yellow**.
* **The Typography:** **CULItrail** matching the suite's font. "CULI" is bright white, and "trail" is the warm saffron yellow.
* **The Sub-headline:** *CULItrail: Map Your Meal. Navigate your meals, orders, and kitchen logistics from pantry to plate.*

### 3. APERtrail: The Photography Journey Planner

* **The Mark:** The icon features a modern, metallic camera aperture. One of the aperture blades seamlessly extends and loops into the suite's signature "S-curve" winding trail, ultimately ascending into a sharp, geometric mountain peak.
* **The Color Palette:** We utilize a crisp, professional aesthetic. The aperture, trail, and mountain are rendered in a sleek **Metallic Silver**, standing out cleanly against the suite's unified dark, textured charcoal background.
* **The Typography:** **APERtrail** matching the suite's clean, sans-serif font. "APER" is bright white, and "trail" is the metallic silver.
* **The Sub-headline:** *APERtrail: Map Your Shot. Connect locations, navigate perspectives.*

### The Unified Ecosystem at a Glance

If you place these three icons on your website or in your Obsidian vault, here is the visual story they tell:

* **The Shape:** All three feature the signature **S-curve trail** integrated into the core object (Aperture, Node, Knife).
* **The Font:** All three use the exact same split-weight typography style (**APERtrail**, **NODAtrail**, **CULItrail**).
* **The Colors:** They are unified by dark charcoal backgrounds, but distinguished by their accent colors:
* **NODAtrail:** Cyan/Purple (High-tech, cognitive, structural)
* **APERtrail:** Silver (Mechanical, outdoor, photographic)
* **CULItrail:** Saffron Yellow (Warm, appetizing, organic)
