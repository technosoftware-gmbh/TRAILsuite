# NODAtrail

**Map your mind.** A life OS for Obsidian: the areas you maintain, the goals
they serve, the projects that advance them, the periodic notes you plan them in,
and the money that belongs to none of it.

## Start here

- [Installation](installation.md), and what a fresh install does to your vault.
- [Usage](usage.md), the everyday workflows.

## Features

- [PARA](features/para.md) - areas, goals, projects, resources and the archive.
- [Plan](features/plan.md) - the five levels of periodic note, and the dialog
  that writes into a day for you.
- [Finance](features/finance.md) - purchases, bills, recurring costs and
  budgets.
- [The ledger](features/ledger.md) - accounts, the journal, the bank statement
  import, and what is owned against what is owed.
